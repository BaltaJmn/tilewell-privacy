Tilewell

Phonics letter tiles that stay where you put them

Tilewell is a letter tile board for iPhone and iPad, for teaching reading and spelling with phonics at home, in tutoring or in class. Drag a tile and it lands where you drop it. Tap it and it says its sound. Build a word and hear it sounded out, then read.

No account, no server of ours. Tilewell opens straight to your board, works with the network turned off and has nothing to download. No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your board is saved on your device after every move. Purchases are recorded by RevenueCat under an anonymous ID (see the privacy policy).

The tiles

The 26 letters, consonant teams (sh, ch, th, ck, ng, tch, dge), vowel teams (ai, ee, igh, oa, ou, oo), r-controlled vowels (ar, er, ir, or, ur), consonant-le syllables, tion, sion and ture, prefixes and suffixes, and a space tile to split syllables. Vowels red, consonants blue, consonant teams green, vowel teams yellow, r-controlled vowels purple. Every page of tiles fits the screen without scrolling, with big tiles on iPad and clear tiles on iPhone. You can also make your own tiles.

The sounds

Every tile says its sounds in a clear American English voice, with an example word: a says /æ/ as in cat, /eɪ/ as in baby, /ɑ/ as in father. Inside a word, each tile says the sound it makes there, so the a in cake says its name and the e is silent. "Sound it out" plays each sound in turn and then the whole word, for real words and made-up ones. Sight words are spelled letter by letter, then read.

The word lists

680 words in 29 lists by phonics pattern: short vowels (CVC), digraphs, blends, soft c and g, tch and dge, silent e, vowel teams, r-controlled vowels, two closed syllables, consonant-le, prefixes, suffixes and the Dolch sight words. Tap a word to build it in tiles, or use spelling mode: the words are hidden and said aloud, and your child builds each one. The lists were written for Tilewell from common English words; the sight words are the Dolch lists, which are in the public domain.

Price

The whole board, every tile, the sounds of the 26 letters and the short a list are free. Tilewell Pro adds the sounds of every team and word part, reading and sounding out words, all 29 lists with spelling mode, and your own tiles. It is a subscription: $14.99 a year or $2.99 a month in the US (prices vary by country), shared with your family through Family Sharing. Cancel anytime in your Apple account settings.
