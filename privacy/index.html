Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short: Tilewell does not collect, store or share any of your data or your child's. Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad.

Your board and your tiles

The tiles on your board, the tiles you make and your settings are saved on your device, in Tilewell's own storage, so the board is there the next time you open the app. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Your device's own iCloud Backup may include them, as it does for other apps. Everything is removed if you delete the app.

The voice

Tilewell uses the voice built into iOS and iPadOS to say sounds and words. It speaks on your device; nothing is recorded and Tilewell never uses the microphone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. Tilewell never sees your name, email or payment details; it only asks Apple whether a subscription is active. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What Tilewell does not do

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code that collects data. Tilewell works with the network turned off.

Children

Tilewell is made for parents, tutors and teachers to use with children. It collects no data from anyone, children included. The subscription screen and every link that leaves the app (this policy, the terms, email) sit behind a parental gate: a question for grown-ups.
