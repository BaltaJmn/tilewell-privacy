Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short: Tilewell does not collect your content or your child's, and has no account, analytics, ads or tracking. Everything you make stays on your iPhone or iPad. Purchases are recorded by RevenueCat with an anonymous identifier (see Purchases).

Your board and your tiles

The tiles on your board, the tiles you make and your settings are saved on your device, in Tilewell's own storage, so the board is there the next time you open the app. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Your device's own iCloud Backup may include them, as it does for other apps. Everything is removed if you delete the app.

The voice

Tilewell uses the voice built into iOS and iPadOS to say sounds and words. It speaks on your device; nothing is recorded and Tilewell never uses the microphone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. Tilewell never sees your name, email or payment details; it asks Apple whether a subscription is active. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

To know, and to restore, whether this installation has Pro, Tilewell uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a processor. RevenueCat receives each in-app purchase you make, assigns the installation an anonymous app user ID, and stores that ID, the purchase history (product, date, price and store transaction IDs) and basic technical device data (OS version, app version, country or storefront). Nothing else from Tilewell, such as your board, your tiles or anything a child does, is sent to RevenueCat or anywhere else. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

To ask for deletion of the purchase record, write to baltajmn@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Tilewell purchase data". Apple keeps its own record of the order under its own policies.

What Tilewell does not do

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The only third-party code in the app is the RevenueCat purchases library, described under Purchases. Tilewell works with the network turned off; the purchase record is sent to RevenueCat when the device is online.

Children

Tilewell is made for parents, tutors and teachers to use with children. It collects no personal data from anyone, children included. The subscription screen and every link that leaves the app (this policy, the terms, email) sit behind a parental gate: a question for grown-ups. Only when a grown-up buys Pro behind that gate does RevenueCat receive anything, and then only an anonymous installation ID and the purchase record (see Purchases). Nothing a child does in the app is sent.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: baltajmn@gmail.com. The developer is Baltasar Jiménez Rincón, Spain (details in the legal notice).
